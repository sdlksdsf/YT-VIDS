The Ottomans now controlled the trade routes to the East. Within a lifetime, Spain was funding a Genoese sailor sailing west, and Portugal was rounding Africa. The age of exploration was, in part, a reaction to the fall of a city. And it created a legend. The Greeks told a story that an angel had rescued Constantine at the last moment, turned him to marble, and sealed him in a cave under the Golden Gate — the Marble Emperor, waiting to wake up and take his city back. It's a beautiful story. It's also a way of saying: this isn't over. Two young men. One city. Fifty-three days. One of them got the city. The other got the legend. And the rest of us got the story.
